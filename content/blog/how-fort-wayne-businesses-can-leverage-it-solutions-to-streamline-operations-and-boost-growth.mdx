Fort Wayne and Northeast Indiana companies run into the same limits. The phones, the files, and the security setup that worked at twenty people start to drag once the company is larger.

## Where the time goes

The day depends on email, file storage, the customer tools, and whoever is watching the network. The slowdowns are usually ordinary:

- Systems old enough to stall a normal day
- Tools that do not talk to each other, so people retype the same information
- Threats the current setup was never built to catch
- No plan for the next hire or the next office

## What 4EOS does

We look at what is already running, name what is wasting time, and write a plan against the goals the company already has.

Managed IT is monitoring, support, and maintenance, so the systems stay up. Cybersecurity is a layered defense for threats that keep changing. Cloud and backup keep the data reachable and restorable. Consulting lines the infrastructure up with the next few years of the business.
